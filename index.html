Ludo

2 to 4 players on one device. Pass the phone around!

How to play

  • Roll a 6 to move a token out of your yard.
  • A 6 gives you another roll. So does capturing.
  • Land on an opponent token to send it back to its yard.
  • Coloured and starred squares are safe — no captures there.
  • You need the exact number to enter home. All 4 tokens home wins.
  • Three 6s in a row and your turn is skipped.
